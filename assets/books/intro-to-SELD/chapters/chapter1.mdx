# Getting Started

## Who is this dictionary for?
This dictionary is primarily intended for people trying to decode and produce Sinhala at all registers. That means people are learning Sinhala as a second or heritage language and hope to be able to speak and or read at all registers. 

## What this means in practice
All of the existing dictionaries are oriented towards people who can already speak Sinhala and need to produce or decode English. As such, they will not include things such as verbal tenses, noun plurals, pronoun variations, register, etc. Therefore these are the very things that must be included in the SELD.
