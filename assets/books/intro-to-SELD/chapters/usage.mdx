# Usage

The two primary usages in the SELD are 

**Literary (lit)**: සාහිත්‍යය or written. Used in government publications, public speeches, news (both written and spoken)  
**Colloquial (col)**: Spoken. The form people generally use for spoken communication outside of specialty discussions. 

Many words will be possible in either context and as such remain unmarked. As a learner seeking the meaning of the word the usage is not as important as when you are trying to actually use a word. Often colloquial and literary words will be linked as synonyms.
