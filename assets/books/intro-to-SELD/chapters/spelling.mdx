# Guessing spelling 

If you are unable to find a word, it may have an alternate spelling or you may simply be spelling it wrong. Sometimes the spelling may indicate a colloquial/literary difference. 

න ↔ ණ  
ල ↔ ළ  
ලු ↔ ළු  
වු ↔ ව්  
ස ↔ හ  
ශ ↔ ෂ

Another common spelling variation relates to spaces. Sinhala is very irregular when it comes to compound verbs and particles being one word or two. Sometimes these are listed as spelling variants, however you may encounter variations that are not noted.
