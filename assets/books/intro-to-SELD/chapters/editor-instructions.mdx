## Headwords & Minor Entries

In general headwords for nouns should be in the singular (nominative/subject) case. Headwords for nouns should be in the infinitive (-නවා form)

In general the more common form of the word should be the headword and the less common alternatives as spelling variants. It’s a judgement call if an alternate form should be a variant (with a minor entry redirecting or not) or a synonym. From the learner’s perspective, it saves time if there is a complete entry for the alternate spelling. From the perspective of space conservation and maintenance, it is better to have a variant with minor entry.

## Usage
When possible, words with one usage should **list the alternate usage as a synonym**.

**(col, lit} should never appear** in an entry. If a word is used in any context, then no specific usage should ever be indicated.

**Not all words** need to have usage information.

**Example sentences should match the usage completely**, meaning that for literary headwords, examples should be completely literary and for colloquial headwords the examples should be completely colloquial. When a word is used in both contexts, try to keep examples colloquial.

Other usages:

**Honoriffic (hon}**: Used in reference to monastics especially but also highly respected people  
**Monastic**: Used exclusively for monks and nuns.  
**Archaic (arch}**: Not used in modern writing or speaking.  
**Old-fashioned (old}**: Generally spoken idioms that have fallen out of modern use.  
**Rural**: ගැමි. Generally only used in villages  
**Slang (slg}**: Only used in informal contexts.
