3. IDIOMATIC EXPRESSIONS

3.1 This conversation introduces a number of idiomatic expressions which are common in everyday social situations. The general tone here is that which would exist in an informal conversation between friends of comparatively equal social status. All of the expressions commented on below may therefore be treated as units and used as such in similarly informal conversations.

3.11 - 'Hello': A term for greeting used by friends on meeting as well as on taking leave, irrespective of the time of day involved. This is the most informal form. මට යන්න ඕන නෑ. I don't want to go.

Emphatic sentences:

මට පුලුවන් සිංහල. It is Sinhalese that I can (speak).

මට බැරි සිංහල It is Sinhalese that I can't (speak).

මට ඕන පොත්. It is books that I want.

මට එපා පොත්. It is books that I don't want.

or

මට ඕන නැත්තෙ පොත්. It is books that I don't want.

මට පුලුවන් යන්න. What I can do is go.

මට බැරි යන්න. What I can't do is go.

මට ඕන යන්න. What I want to do is go.

මට ඕන නැත්තෙ යන්න. What I don't want to do is go.

Positive

12.2 කැමති

Constructions

Simple sentences:

Positive

මම පොතට කැමතියි.

I like the book.

මට යන්න කැමතියි.

I like to go.

Emphatic sentences:

මම කැමති පොතට.

It is the book that I like.

මම කැමති යන්න.

What I like to do is go.

Negative

මම පොතට කැමති නෑ.

I don't like the book.

මම යන්න කැමති නෑ.

I don't like to go.

මම කැමති නැත්තෙ පොතට.

What I don't like is the book.

මම කැමති නැත්තෙ යන්න.

What I don't like to do is go.

12.3 ඉන්නවා and තියෙනවා

Constructions

1. With Dative Case Subjects:

Positive

Negative

I don't have a son.

I don't have a book.

Positive

It is a son that I have.

මට තියෙන්නෙ පොතක්.

Negative
