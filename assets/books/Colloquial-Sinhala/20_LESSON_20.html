3.1 The form a may equate with the English 'as much as', 'as many as', 'as well as'. This use of විතර is confined to questions or negative sentences. The two forms being compared will appear in the same case, as the sentence requires:

මට මේ ලමයාට විතර සිංහල බෑ.

I don't know Sinhalese as well as this boy.

and ලමයාට in is dative. In the sentence above the form subject of dative because is in the dative case as the expression ලමයාට විතර Note also the cases in the 1s

following:

8. 'GLAD, PLEASED'

8.1 Expressions such as a 'glad' or 'pleased' and 'glad', 'like' may be used with a dependent noun or pronoun in the dative case:

මම නැටුම් වලට කැමතියි.

මම ඒකට සන්තෝසයි.

I like dances.

I am pleased about it.

8.2 The same forms may be used with a dependent infinitive equivalent to English 'glad to', 'pleased to', although this construction may only be used if the subject of ຜຜ or ໑໖ is identical with the subject of the infinitive.

මම ඒ පලාතට යන්න කැමතියි.

I like to go to that area.

In this construction, the student is advised to use only, because ຜ is more rarely used.

8.3 Likewise, the same expressions may take a simple present or simple past tense in the dative case (i.e., with added to the simple present or past). In this usage the subject of or සන්තොස may or may not be the same as the subject of the dependent verb. If they are the same, the construction may



[page 300]

equate with the English 'glad to', 'pleased to', or 'glad that', 'pleased that', and is an alternative to the construction with an infinitive. In this construction, the student is advised to use only සන්තෝස since කැමති 1s more rarely used. If the subject of ຜ or ໑໖ is different from the subject of the dependent verb, then the construction will equate with English 'glad that', 'pleased that' and in this case only this construction and not the one with the infinitive is used:

මම මහත්තයා හම්බ උනාට සන්තෝසයි.

I'm glad to have met you.

or

I'm glad that you are coming.

12. ඇති

12.1 The form 48 'might be', 'must be may be used with adjectives:

This work must be difficult.

12.24 may also be added to a sentence with the verb in the simple present form:
