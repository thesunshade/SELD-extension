3. IDIOMS

In this conversation there are some variants of items learned earlier as well as some new idiomatic expressions.

3.1 ආ බෝවන් is a variant of the greeting ආයිබෝ 'Hello', 'how do you do? It is a slightly more formal and literary version and may be used when first meeting someone, as in this conversa-tion, or may be used as a term of respect where ආයිබෝ would imply more familiarity. Note that at the end of the conversation the form ໖໖ is used since the participants have become ac-quainted with each other and have been chatting informally.

It has two 3.2 වහත්මයා is a more formal variant of මහත්තයා plural forms මහත්වරු and මහත්මයාලා. මහත්මයාලා is used more fre-quently as a term of address or equivalent of 'you (pl.)' and ໙໖໖, generally as an equivalent of 'gentlemen'.

3.3 අපට is a variant of අපිට the dative of 8. Either may be used with no change in meaning.

3.4 අපි ගිහින් එන්නම් is a polite expression used on parting by the person who is leaving. It means, literally, 'we, having gone, will come and is a rough equivalent of English 'I, we, had better

6. THE GOD SET

6.1 මෙච්චර

'this much' is one of another set of location words:

මෙච්චර ඔච්චර අවර

this much, as much as this

that much (by you), as much as that (by you)

that much (over there), as much as that (over there)

that much (in question), as much as that (in question)

These have no case forms.

6.2 Members of the Gedo set may modify nouns, and then may either precede or follow the noun they qualify:

එව්වර සල්ලි වියදම් උනාද?

I don't want this much food.

මට කෑම මෙච්චර එපා. මාසෙකින් කොහොම ද ඔව්වර සිංහල ඉගන ගත්තේ?

Did that much money get spent? How did you learn that much Sinhalese in a month?

6.3 They may also act as nouns:

මහත්තයාට ඔව්වර බොන්න පුලුවන්ද?

Can you drink that much?

6.4 They may modify adjectives or verbs:

මේ සුරුට්ටු එව්වර රස නෑ. ඒ ලමයාගෙ වැඩ මෙවර හොඳ නෑ.

These cigars aren't that tasty. That boy's work isn't this good.

ඇමෙරිකාවෙ කෑම ඔච්චර කට දනවා ද?
